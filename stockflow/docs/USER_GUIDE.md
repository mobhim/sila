# User guide

## Employees (phone)
1. Open StockFlow → you land on **Record movement**. Your branch is selected automatically.
2. Choose the movement: **Consumption**, **Withdrawal** or **Return**.
3. Tap a recent/favourite product, search by name/SKU, or tap **Scan** and point the camera at the
   barcode. (A USB/Bluetooth scanner also works: scan into the search box.)
4. Enter the quantity (switch unit, e.g. carton, if the product has one) → **Continue** → **Confirm**.
   You'll see the new balance and a TX number.
* Mistake? Entries can't be edited. Use **Report a problem** (damaged, expired, missing) — your
  manager approves it, then the stock is corrected.
* **My history** shows everything you recorded and the status of your reports.
* Stock counts: open **Stock counts**, pick the open count, enter what you physically counted.

## Branch managers
* **Dashboard / Inventory** — your branches only. Inventory tabs: needs attention, low, critical, out.
* **Alerts** — acknowledge, or create a replenishment request from an alert.
* **Replenishment** — review auto-drafts (each line explains its recommended quantity), adjust,
  approve, send to the supplier, and **receive** deliveries (partial receipts allowed; attach the
  delivery note). Close short if the rest will never arrive.
* **Transfers** — request stock from another branch or send to one. Stock leaves on *dispatch*
  and arrives on *receive*.
* **Stock counts** — create, let staff count, submit (every difference needs a reason), approve.
* **Adjustments** — approve or reject employee reports.
* **Reports** — CSV export or *PDF* (print view → "Save as PDF").

## Suppliers (portal)
* You see only requests sent to your company.
* **Accept** (with expected delivery date) or **decline** (with reason).
* Mark **In transit**, confirm the quantities you are shipping, upload the delivery note/invoice.
* The branch records what actually arrived; you'll be notified of receipts.
